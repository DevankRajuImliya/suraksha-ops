## Coal Mine ERP & Safety Management System

A web-based ERP and safety management system designed specifically for **coal mining operations**. The platform helps mine workers and management efficiently manage employee records, documents, workplace information, and safety-related activities through a centralized digital system.

The system enables **miners and employees to report workplace hazards, safety concerns, incidents, and operational problems directly through the platform**. These reports are routed to the **Mine Manager or authorized management personnel**, allowing them to review, track, assign, and manage issues until they are resolved.

### Key Features

* 👷 **Employee & Miner Record Management** – Maintain organized digital records of mine workers and staff.
* 📁 **Document & File Management** – Store, organize, and manage important mine-related documents and records.
* ⚠️ **Hazard Reporting** – Allow workers to quickly report hazards and unsafe conditions at the workplace.
* 🚨 **Incident & Problem Reporting** – Report accidents, equipment issues, operational problems, and other workplace concerns.
* 👨‍💼 **Mine Manager Dashboard** – Provide management with a centralized view of reported issues and mine activities.
* 📊 **Issue Tracking & Management** – Track reported problems from submission through resolution.
* 🔔 **Notifications & Status Updates** – Keep relevant personnel informed about reported hazards and their progress.
* 🔐 **Role-Based Access Control** – Provide different levels of access for miners, supervisors, managers, and administrators.
* 🗃️ **Centralized Data Management** – Maintain mine records and safety information in one secure digital platform.

### Objective

The primary objective of this project is to **digitize and streamline administrative and safety-management processes in coal mines**, reducing dependency on manual paperwork while improving communication between workers and management.

By providing a direct channel for workers to report workplace hazards and operational problems, the system aims to support **faster issue identification, better record management, improved accountability, and more organized mine operations**.
